A bit about me.

I write production software, design electrical control systems, and build hardware that moves the world. For two years I worked as an Engineering Co-op at Tenneco Inc., a Tier 1 automotive manufacturer — self-teaching AutoCAD Electrical, programming PLC systems in Structured Text and Ladder Logic, developing HMI/SCADA dashboards, commissioning custom automated machinery, and integrating Cognex vision systems and Epson robots into live production lines. My most complex schematics became the new standard in the plant.

On the software side, I built MoneyMate — a full-stack AI application using OpenAI API and OCR that achieves 95% extraction accuracy — and SilentBell, an IoT hardware system I designed, wired, and fabricated from scratch using AutoCAD Electrical, SolidWorks, and 3D printing. I graduated from the University of Toledo with a 3.9 GPA and Magna Cum Laude honors.

Outside engineering, I'm a passionate cook — my family owns a restaurant back home in Nepal and I grew up alongside a father who is a genuinely great chef. I'm a Manchester United supporter, an active soccer player, and someone who has spoken four languages since childhood. I've lived in Nepal, visited India, Korea, and Qatar, and I am now building my career in the United States.
